Our Story

A Warm Welcome
Every Time You Stay

Nestled under a canopy of majestic oak trees, Three Oaks Motel has been a beloved retreat for travelers passing through Titusville, Florida. Whether you're here to witness a rocket launch at nearby Kennedy Space Center or simply exploring Florida's scenic Space Coast, we offer the comfort and hospitality you deserve.

We pride ourselves on clean, well-appointed rooms, friendly 24-hour service, and an atmosphere that feels like home. From the moment you arrive to the moment you check out — your comfort is our priority.
